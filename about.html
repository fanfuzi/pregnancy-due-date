About Pregnancy Calculator Hub

Welcome to Pregnancy Calculator Hub, an independent project that builds free, easy-to-use pregnancy tools and plain-language guides for expecting parents.

Who We Are

We are a small editorial team that researches, writes, and maintains every calculator and article on this site. Our focus is on making commonly asked pregnancy questions — "when is my due date?", "what happens each trimester?", "what should I pack for the hospital?" — clear and accessible.

How We Create and Review Content

Every article and tool on this site is written and checked by our editorial team. Our process is:

  • Research topics against current public-health guidance and reputable medical sources.
  • Write in plain language and label anything that is an estimate or a general guideline.
  • Review for accuracy and keep dates visible so you know when content was last updated.
  • Add a medical disclaimer where information could be mistaken for medical advice.

We are not a substitute for your doctor or midwife. No content on this site is personalized medical advice.

Sources We Rely On

Our content is informed by guidance from established health organizations, including:

  • NHS — Pregnancy
  • ACOG — American College of Obstetricians and Gynecologists
  • CDC — Pregnancy
  • WHO — Pregnancy

Important Medical Disclaimer

This website provides general information and tools for educational purposes only. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Estimated due dates are approximate — only about 4% of babies are born on their exact due date. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider for decisions about your pregnancy and health.
