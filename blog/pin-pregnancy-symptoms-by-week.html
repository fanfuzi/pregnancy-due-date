Pregnancy Symptoms
by Week

What to expect in each trimester
🤢 First Trimester (Weeks 1-12)
Morning sickness peaks around week 9. Fatigue, breast tenderness, and frequent urination are common.
🌟 Second Trimester (Weeks 13-26)
Energy returns! You may feel first movements (quickening) around week 16-20. Back pain and round ligament pain may start.
👶 Third Trimester (Weeks 27-40+)
Shortness of breath, Braxton Hicks contractions, pelvic pressure, and nesting instinct. Baby drops around week 36.
