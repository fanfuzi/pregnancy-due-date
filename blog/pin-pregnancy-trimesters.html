Trimesters

What to expect in each stage
🤰 First Trimester
Weeks 1-12
Major organs form. Morning sickness, fatigue, and breast tenderness are common.
👶 Second Trimester
Weeks 13-26
Energy returns. You may feel first movements. The "golden trimester" for many moms.
🌟 Third Trimester
Weeks 27-40+
Rapid growth. Shortness of breath, Braxton Hicks, and nesting instinct kick in.
